About Safa Marwa Cattle Farm

Safa Marwa Cattle Farm livestock trading aur high-quality breeding me ek mutabar aur jana-pehchana naam hai. Hamara maqsad pure Pakistan me livestock ke shaukeen hazraat ko nihayat sehat-mand, khubsurat aur bhaari bharkam naslo ke maveshi faraham karna hai.

Hamare farm par har janwar ki khorak, saaf-suthray mahool aur medical healthcare ka bohot baray paimane par khyal rakha jata hai. Hamari makhsoos naslein jisme Brahman, Cholistani, Sahiwal, aur heavy cargo bulls shamil hain, apni behtareen body structure aur weight ke liye jani jati hain.

Aap poore aitemad ke sath hamare inventory setup se online booking kar sakte hain ya hamare stall setup par tashreef la kar apni pasand ka nayab janwar chun sakte hain.

Palai Services

Apna Janwar Hamare Paas Amanat Rakhein

Palai service un customers ke liye hai jo apna khud ka janwar khareed kar Eid tak hamare farm par palwana chahtay hain. Hamari trained team roz khorak, safai aur health checkup ka poora khayal rakhti hai, taake aapka janwar Eid tak healthy aur bhaari ho jaye.

2027-28 season ke liye Palai booking open hai — Golden aur Platinum, dono packages available hain, jin me desi wanda, kuttar, bhusa, vaccination aur medical checkup shamil hain.
